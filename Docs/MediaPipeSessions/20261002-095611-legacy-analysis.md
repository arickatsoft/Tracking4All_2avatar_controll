## Main finding

The camera averaged **52.4 FPS**, while **1,069 new poses in 112.02 seconds** reached the avatar: **9.54 poses/second**. All evaluated movement detections were accepted, and the recorder dropped **zero records**. Continuous confidence/availability does not mean the pose is accurate or every rapid action was sampled. The avatar receives a new measurement approximately every **101 ms**; a fast extension/retraction can occur between measurements.

| Measured stage | Median ms | 95th percentile ms |
|---|---:|---:|
| Frame available → accepted pose | 101.34 | 120.43 |
| Frame available → first avatar application | 102.24 | 122.47 |
| Native inference call | 48.65 | 57.26 |
| Scheduling → GPU readback callback | 48.17 | 49.73 |
| GPU callback → result consumption (includes native inference) | 51.92 | 68.80 |

Readback elapsed time includes GPU/driver scheduling and callback delivery; it is not 48 ms of measured shader execution. The callback-to-consumption interval already contains inference and must not be added again to compute. Medians of different stages are not an exact sum. Camera exposure/buffering before Unity and display latency are excluded. Screen recording and diagnostic writing were active; keep those conditions the same for the paired run.

The current code serializes readback then CPU inference for each frame (`RTMPosePoseProvider.Tick`/`ConsumeResult`), with no inference request queue. About half the measured delay occurs before inference. The first confirmed optimization target after the paired test is camera conversion/readback scheduling, followed by native execution. Per-region smoothing alone cannot remove this wait or turn 9.5 new poses/second into 52.

## Motion and stillness

- Arms sideways/overhead, elbow movements, forward/side torso bends, knee lifts and squats are visible in the preview/recorded skeleton and produce avatar movement. This is qualitative evidence, not an accuracy score or proof that every repetition was captured.
- Matched source/first-avatar records preserve processed elbow bend angles closely: median absolute difference **0.39° left / 0.43° right**, p95 **7.14° / 6.86°**. Knee bend differences have median approximately **0°**, p95 **2.90° left / 2.45° right**. These compare the processed skeleton to the avatar, not either to the human. Direction/depth errors can remain despite matching bend angles.
- Left/right knee-lift segments show raw-model knee bend p95 around **127° / 125°**, processed around **129° / 128°**, avatar around **129° / 129°**. Squat knee bends also reach the avatar. There is no evidence here of the previous 2D solver discarding the entire calibrated MediaPipe pose.
- At initial rest, calibrated raw wrist/knee RMS was **1.08 cm**, filtered **0.60 cm** (about **44% lower**). At final rest, raw **2.16 cm**, filtered **1.89 cm**. The larger final variation includes model drift and any real movement; it cannot all be labeled noise. This metric covers four joints, not the entire body.
- Brief wrist occlusion reduced left-wrist availability to **96.1%**. Other reported wrist/knee availability was 100%. The short confidence drop does not prove anatomically correct tracking behind the body.
- Fast-punch frames show directions that warrant detailed labeling (including raised avatar arms). The low sample cadence, model depth estimates, calibration orientation, and filtering/prediction can each contribute. This run alone does not isolate the cause. Raw/filtered joint data is saved so the next investigation can distinguish these stages.

The on-phone movement tables assign pose and avatar samples at slightly different render/cue boundaries; some avatar rows are one sample higher/lower than accepted rows. Globally there are exactly **1,069 unique avatar sequence IDs**, so this is a cue-bucketing artifact, not duplicate inference or avatar motion. This review pairs samples by source sequence to avoid that artifact.

The first still cue appears around video **8 s**, and the side-bend cue around **56 s**, matching session cue offsets **10.25 s** and **58.27 s**. Approximate mapping: **video time = session elapsed − 2.25 s**, with roughly a quarter-second uncertainty from sampled UI transitions. Cue text marks speech enqueue, not audible onset or action start. Do not align these files just by their starting frames or durations.

The preview shows a low camera looking upward with head/feet in frame. Phone gravity estimated **64.65°** pitch, while the UI/manual field was clamped to **45°**. The new MediaPipe world path derives its orientation from the standing 3D reference, rather than using this field as RTMPose's 2D perspective correction. The mismatch is a diagnostics limitation, not established as the cause of delay. Keep placement unchanged for the paired filtering run.
